    Mum, you turned every one of my birthdays into a party. Today it’s my turn: I picked my favourite photos and a song that reminds me of our kitchen. Love you.

    Swap “our kitchen” for the place that reminds you of each other.

Frequently asked questions

Before you light the candles.

What is the 2luv digital birthday gift?

A surprise link that opens a party: a 3D cake, balloons with their age, your letter, photos, music and games like a claw machine and a photo booth. The birthday person blows out the candles through the phone’s microphone.

What can I see before I pay?

You can build the whole gift and watch the live preview for free. The payment is a one-time charge in your local currency that unlocks the full experience, with the link and QR code to send it.

What is the difference between the Birthday Party and the Birthday Letter?

The Birthday Party is the cinematic gift, with 3D scenes, games and sound. The Birthday Letter is the classic letter with a countdown, cake and confetti. Both take photos, music and places.

Are eCards included?

No. eCards are a separate option: you can take the letter with a collection of interactive eCards in a single payment, with the Letter + eCards plan.

Can I send it before the day?

Yes. The gift shows a countdown to midnight on the birthday, in their time zone, and celebrates when the day arrives.

Does the recipient need an app?

No. The gift opens in the browser of any phone, tablet or computer, with no sign-up and no login.

Does the microphone record anything?

No. The breath is detected on the device and nothing is recorded or sent. Anyone who prefers can hold a button to blow out the candles.

How do I send it?

You get a link and a QR code. Send it on WhatsApp, Instagram or email, or print the QR code and put it on a card or next to a present.

Can I edit it after sending?

Yes. Once it is unlocked you can change photos, text and moments, and the link stays the same.
