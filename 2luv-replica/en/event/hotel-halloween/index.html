Frequently asked questions

Before you open the envelope.

How much does it cost? Is it a subscription?+

It’s not a subscription. It’s a one-time payment, shown on this page in your local currency. No monthly fees, no ads.

What does the recipient get?+

A link (or a QR code) that opens a sealed envelope with their name. Then comes a Halloween reception with up to 8 moments: your letter, photos, music, your special date, dreams, the chair, a tarot reading with your message and a screening room with the film you suggested.

Do I need to download an app?+

No. Everything opens in the browser, for you and for the recipient.

Does it work on phones?+

Yes. The gift adapts to phones, tablets and computers, in portrait or landscape.

Can I edit it after sending?+

Yes. Once it’s unlocked, you can adjust the gift and keep sharing it.

Is it private?+

Each gift has an exclusive link and can be password-protected.

How long does it take to create?+

It depends on how much you want to write. The live preview shows the result as you build.

How do I send it?+

When you finish, you get the link. Send it via WhatsApp, Instagram, email or any messaging app — or print the QR code and tuck it into a card or alongside a physical gift.
