Frequently asked questions

What is 2Luv?

2Luv is a platform for creating private digital love letters and personalized interactive cards. You can combine messages, photos, music, and memorable moments in one shareable link.

How does the Letter unlock work?

You create the Letter first, preview the cover, and complete a one-time payment to open the full content. The access stays tied to that Letter.

What are Interactive Cards?

Interactive Cards are optional add-ons for the Letter, such as countdowns, timelines, star maps, photo galleries, word searches, quizzes, and more.

How much does it cost and how does payment work?

The current Letter and Cards prices appear before checkout. You can unlock only the Letter or add Cards with the bundle discount when available. Payment is one-time, with no monthly subscription.

How long does my link stay available?

After unlock, the Letter link remains available without an expiration date, so the recipient can revisit the surprise whenever they want.

Can I edit my letter after creating it?

Yes. You can update the Letter and its details from the same creation/editing flow whenever you need to adjust the surprise.
