Frequently asked questions

Planning your digital gift.

Is this a physical gift?

The gift is a digital Letter with your message and memories. There is no parcel or postal delivery; you can print the QR code yourself.

Can I send it as soon as it is ready?

Once the full Letter is unlocked, you can share its link yourself. Check your text, photos and recipient before sending it.

Does the recipient need an app?

The Letter opens in a browser on a phone or computer. Review your chosen viewing access option before sharing it.

Can I make the gift on my phone?

You can use the creator in your phone’s browser, add your photos and message, and check the preview before sharing.

What do I pay for?

A one-time payment unlocks the full Letter. The regional price is shown before payment. Interactive eCards are a separate upgrade.

How do I keep it personal?

Keep the link private and send it only to your chosen recipient. Choose the viewing access option in the creator and share any required access details separately.
