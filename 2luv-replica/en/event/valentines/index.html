    Frequently asked questions

    Before you send your Valentine's letter.

    What should I write in a Valentine's Day letter?

    Start with a memory only you share, name something you appreciate about them and add a photo or song that brings it back. Your own words make the gift personal.

    Can I prepare it before Valentine's Day?

    Yes. Create and review your letter ahead of time. Once unlocked, keep the link and send it yourself when you want them to open it; delivery is not scheduled automatically.

    Are interactive eCards included in the Valentine's letter?

    Interactive eCards are a separate upgrade. Choose the Letter on its own or review the Letter + eCards option and its regional price before paying.

    How much does it cost?

    Building it and seeing the preview is free. To unlock it you make a one-time payment, shown on this page in your local currency. It is not a subscription.

    Do they need to download an app?

    No. The letter opens in the browser of any phone, tablet or computer, with no sign-up and no login.

    How do I send the letter?

    When you unlock it you get a link and a QR code. Send it on WhatsApp, Instagram, email or text, or print the QR code and put it on a card, in a bouquet or next to a present.

    Can I see what it looks like before paying?

    Yes. The live preview shows the gift exactly as they will see it while you build it.

    Is the letter private?

    Yes. Every letter has its own private link, can be password-protected and never shows up in search engines.

    Can I edit it after sending?

    Yes. Once it is unlocked you can change the text, photos and moments, and the same link keeps working.

    How many photos can I add?

    Up to 10 photos per letter, each with an optional caption and date.
