FAQ

About your digital Christmas gift

What is a digital Christmas gift on 2luv?

It is a personalized Christmas letter inside an interactive experience. You bring messages, photos and music together in a Christmas room that the recipient explores through the gift link.

Can I create a virtual Christmas card with photos and music?

Yes. You can personalize the Letter with text, photos and a supported song. The Christmas theme presents them in interactive scenes, such as the letter, the tree and the memory train.

Who can I send this online Christmas gift to?

You can make the surprise for your partner, family members or friends. Choose words and photos that mean something to that person and leave out any moments you do not want to fill in.

How can I send a Christmas gift from a distance?

Once you have prepared and unlocked the Letter, share its link through your messaging app or send the QR code. The recipient opens it in a browser without installing the 2luv app.

Is creating and sending a Christmas letter free?

You can prepare the Letter and check its preview before paying. Opening the full content requires a one-time payment. The current price appears on this page and before checkout; interactive Cards are optional add-ons.

Can I edit my Christmas gift later?

Yes. Use your Letter’s editing flow to update the text, photos and details. If it is protected, you will need to enter the editing password.
