Help with your gift

Personalize, preview and share

Which design should I choose?

Choose a Love Letter for a romantic message or a Birthday Letter for birthday wishes. The cinematic designs present your memories in themed scenes; the available choices appear above. Open an example to understand a design before starting. A locked design keeps its opening notice and cannot be started from its card while it is unavailable.

How do I make it personal?

In the letter creator, start with the recipient’s name and add your signature where offered. In Memories, write a message in your own words, choose photographs and paste a supported song link. The design may offer more details to personalize. Use photos you want the recipient to see, check their framing and review your spelling before moving on.

What should I check in the preview?

Use the preview to check your message, photo framing and music before finishing. On a phone, the preview button opens the gift separately from the form. An example contains demonstration names, photographs and text; it shows the design rather than your own content. Close it to return to the form, and use your own preview to review what you have entered. “Use this example” fills the editor with sample content. If your draft has content, confirm before replacing it; “Undo example” restores it while you stay on the page.

How do I send the finished gift?

Finish the creation steps and follow the options shown to make your gift ready to share. When the sharing options are available, send the link in a message or email, or use the QR code for a printed card. Keep the link and access details displayed to you. If you configured a password, send it separately to the recipient.
